About MOMO Noise

MOMO Noise is a browser tool for making sound-effect buttons.

Record from your mic or clip a loaded audio file, and turn it into a button that plays when pressed. It trims silence automatically, adjusts pitch, and plays in sync with a metronome. Save and load your button sets with Google Drive.

It runs right in your phone's browser. No installation needed.
